Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 3, 2026

Welcome to 25sec ("the App", "we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our interval timer mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

The 25sec app does not require user registration. We do not collect personal information such as name, email address, or phone number.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

The following information may be collected automatically:

  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, language settings
  • App Usage Data: Feature usage, session duration, crash reports
  • Advertising Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID) for ad delivery

1.3 Locally Stored Data

The following information is stored only on your device and is not transmitted to external servers:

  • Timer routine settings
  • App preferences (language, theme)
  • Subscription status

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • Providing and improving app functionality
  • Displaying personalized or non-personalized advertisements
  • Processing subscription payments (via Google Play)
  • Analyzing crashes and bugs
  • Improving user experience

3. Third-Party Services

The 25sec app uses the following third-party services:

3.1 Google AdMob (Advertising)

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect the following information:

  • Advertising identifiers
  • IP address
  • Device and app information

For more information, please see Google's Privacy Policy.

3.2 Google Play Billing (Payments)

Subscription payments are processed through Google Play. Payment information is managed by Google.

3.3 RevenueCat (Subscription Management)

We use RevenueCat to manage subscription status. RevenueCat may collect anonymous subscription data.

For more information, please see RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

4. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, and Switzerland, we process personal data based on the following legal grounds:

  • Consent: For personalized advertising and analytics
  • Legitimate Interest: For app improvement and security
  • Contract Performance: For subscription services

5. Your Rights (GDPR)

Users in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland have the following rights:

  • Right of Access: Request access to your personal data
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your data ("Right to be Forgotten")
  • Right to Restrict Processing: Request restriction of data processing
  • Right to Data Portability: Receive your data in a portable format
  • Right to Object: Object to data processing
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise these rights, please contact us at the email address provided below.

6. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

California residents have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

  • Right to know what personal information is collected
  • Right to delete personal information
  • Right to opt-out of the sale of personal information
  • Right to non-discrimination

The 25sec app does not sell personal information.

7. Children's Privacy

The 25sec app is not intended for children under the age of 13 (or 16 in some jurisdictions). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child, please contact us immediately.
