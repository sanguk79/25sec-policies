Data Deletion Request

Last Updated: June 3, 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your data from the 25sec app.

What Data Does 25sec Store?

Locally Stored Data (Device Only)

  • Timer routine settings
  • App preferences (language, theme)
  • Subscription status cache

Note: This data is stored only on your device and is automatically deleted when you uninstall the app.

Third-Party Service Data

  • Google AdMob: Advertising identifiers (managed by Google)
  • Google Play: Subscription transaction history (managed by Google)
  • RevenueCat: Anonymous subscription status

How to Delete Your Data

Method 1: Uninstall the App

The simplest way to delete locally stored data:

  1. Long-press the 25sec app icon
  2. Select "Uninstall"
  3. All local data will be permanently deleted

Method 2: Reset Advertising Identifier

To reset your advertising identifier:

  1. Open device Settings
  2. Go to Google → Ads
  3. Tap "Reset advertising ID"

Method 3: Email Request

To request deletion of all data including third-party services:

Email: 25sec.support@gmail.com

Required Information:

  • Subject: "Data Deletion Request"
  • Device type (Android/iOS)
  • Approximate date you installed the app
  • Reason for deletion (optional)

Processing Time: Within 30 days (per GDPR requirements)

Subscription Cancellation

To cancel your subscription:

  1. Open Google Play Store
  2. Tap your profile icon → Payments & subscriptions
  3. Select Subscriptions
  4. Find 25sec and tap Cancel subscription

Contact

For any questions about data deletion, please contact:

  • Email: 25sec.support@gmail.com
